# MIRAGE Research Charter

## Scope

- Candidate study: selective caller-state projection into coding-agent tool
  executors, and when it makes local validation non-transferable to a declared
  target environment.
- Target EAIT Track 2: Autonomous Agents. Human-agent trust is secondary.
- Exclude generic prompt injection, RCE, secret access, and production-security claims.

## Integrity

- No fabricated data, citations, experiments, figures, baselines, or claims.
- Every non-trivial claim needs a verified source or versioned experiment output.
- Cite only sources with checked title, authors, venue/status, year, and canonical URL or DOI.
- Label preprints as preprints. Do not claim "first" without a documented novelty audit.
- Report negative results, failure cases, and limitations.

## Experimental Protocol

- Freeze plane fixtures, state categories, prompts, metrics, and controls before headline runs.
- Log later protocol changes with reason and rerun affected comparisons.
- Require natural-readiness baseline, clean controls, divergent controls, target-plane
  oracle, and cost measurement.
- Preserve commands, configs, source/input hashes, outputs, logs, and environment metadata.
- Reproduce every headline table from clean checkout before submission.

## Submission Gates

- EAIT WIP: submit only with reproducible result table and bounded claim by Jul 30, 2026.
- Failed gate means skip or pivot. Never fill deadline with speculative prose.
- Do not submit materially overlapping manuscript to another venue.
- Stage 0 is calibration only. It cannot support a WIP claim unless a frozen
  multi-fixture, multi-agent protocol and refreshed novelty audit pass.
- SIEVE Stage 1 v1 stopped at its cross-product execution gate. The corrected
  v2 protocol also stopped at Trial 1 because Claude had no authenticated
  model session under its required `--bare` configuration. See
  `docs/sieve-stage1-v2-result.md`. No more SIEVE agent runs, protocol
  changes, or paper drafting without fresh explicit consent.

## Working Rules

- Work only in this `mirage` repository. Leave `mirage-local` untouched.
- MIRAGE, PRISM, and SCOPE records are negative calibrations. They cannot
  support a new paper claim.
- User owns scientific decisions, author decisions, and final submission.
- AI is not an author. Check EAIT policy and disclose AI use if required before submission.
